# Board summary: Coast

**Vote: 2 FUND IF, 1 PASS · average score 3.7 / 10**
(Offers lens 4 FUND IF · Monopoly lens 3 PASS · Product lens 4 FUND IF). Memos in `board/`.

## Risks more than one member raised (most dangerous first)

1. **Unverified proof on the website (all three).** The "10x faster" / "50+ brands" stats and the three named testimonials have no stated source. If they are not real customers, that is a trust and consumer-protection problem with exactly the buyers who will notice. Fix before any marketing spend.
2. **Commodity vs. a general AI assistant (all three).** A freelancer can get most of Coast from ChatGPT/Claude with one saved brief per client for ~$20/month, and schedulers (Buffer, SocialBee, Metricool, Ocoya, Predis) already bundle AI writing. No 10x edge today.
3. **"Unlimited AI" at a flat fee with unknown cost per request (Offers, Monopoly).** Heavy Agency users could cost more than €99. Needs a number from the CFO.
4. **The workflow stops before the job ends (Product, Offers, Monopoly).** No client approvals (marked "future"), no publishing/scheduler hand-off. Coast is "one more tab".
5. **No distribution plan / unknown CAC (Monopoly, Offers).**
6. **Touchpoint polish (Product):** Bolt subdomain containing "dvai-rebranding", Gmail contact, 2-day trial too short to judge voice quality.

## The strongest version the board can see

Not quite what is pitched. The strongest Coast is **not** "an AI caption writer for agencies" (a commodity) but **the client-approval-and-voice layer for small agencies in a market the big US tools serve badly**: a per-client voice that is *proven* (edit rate shown), a client approval link so the agency's customer signs off inside Coast, and an export to whatever scheduler the agency already uses. Start in one narrow, reachable market — e.g. Baltic / Lithuanian-language agencies, where English-first tools write weakly — own it, then expand. That version answers the commodity risk (language + approval workflow), the seam risk, and gives a distribution story (a small, dense community). The Monopoly lens would still want proof of share before funding.

The board disagrees on whether there is any business here: the Offers and Product lenses see a fixable offer; the Monopoly lens sees no secret and passes. That disagreement stands.
