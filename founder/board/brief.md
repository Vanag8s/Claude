# Board brief: Coast

## The idea
- What it is: an AI content SaaS that turns a short brand brief into captions, post ideas and video scripts in each client's own voice, with a per-client content calendar to review, approve and schedule.
- Who it is for: social media agencies and freelance social media managers who run content for several client brands at once (from solo freelancers to ~20-person teams).
- What it sells, at what price: monthly subscription. Free trial 2 days, no card, up to 3 clients. Starter €19/month (up to 5 client profiles), Pro €49/month (up to 20, priority response speed), Agency €99/month (unlimited client profiles; "team seats & approvals" marked as future). All paid plans: unlimited AI content requests, per-client calendar, one-click copy and save.
- Where and how: online web app, priced in euros (Europe-facing; site imagery references the dunes of Nida, Lithuania). Built on the Bolt app builder, with sign-in and row-level security. Acquisition channel not stated.
- Budget and constraints: not stated. Business stage not stated.

## What the landing page says (founder's site copy, summarised)
- Headline: "Coast through your content. Every client, every platform." Positioned as "built for agencies managing multiple brands", versus "typical AI social tools built for a single brand".
- Features: chat assistant that reads each client's saved voice (tone, audience, words to use and avoid); switch between client workspaces; per-client content calendar with draft/scheduled/published status; copy a reply or save it to the calendar in one click; data private per account.
- Coast does NOT publish to social platforms itself: the user copies content into their own scheduler ("Coast sits between your brief and your scheduler"). The calendar is for tracking.
- How it works: add a client (5 minutes), brief the assistant in chat, save to the calendar and schedule.
- Claims on the page: "10x faster content production per client", "50+ brands managed from one workspace", "5 min to set up a new client profile".
- Three testimonials (Laura M., Seaside Social; Tomas K., freelancer; Priya S., Brightwave Agency). Note for the board: the founder has not said whether these testimonials and figures come from real customers; treat them as unverified.
- Contact is a Gmail address. Human review stays in the loop; AI writes the first draft.

## Unknowns the board should name rather than guess
Number of users or paying customers, AI/API cost per request (relevant because requests are unlimited on every paid plan), acquisition channel and cost, founder budget and time, team size.
