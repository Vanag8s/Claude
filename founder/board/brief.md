# Coast

- What it is: an AI content workspace for social media agencies that turns a short per-client brand brief into captions, post ideas and video scripts in each client's voice, with a per-client content calendar.
- Who it is for: small social media agencies and freelance social media managers who run content for several client brands at once (roughly 3 to 50 brands), mainly in Europe (euro pricing).
- What it sells, at what price: a monthly SaaS subscription. Free trial 2 days, no card, up to 3 clients. Starter €19/month (5 clients), Pro €49/month (20 clients, "most popular"), Agency €99/month (unlimited clients; team seats and approvals listed as "future"). Unlimited AI requests on every paid plan.
- Where and how: online, self-serve web app (built on Bolt, currently at dvai-rebranding-and-2jqh.bolt.host; contact coastagencyai@gmail.com). It does not post to social networks itself: users copy the text into their own scheduler or save it to Coast's calendar. Brand story references Nida, Lithuania.
- Budget and constraints: not stated by the founder. Not yet known: marketing budget, the AI model and its per-request cost, current users or revenue, team size. The site shows three testimonials and stats ("10x faster", "50+ brands") whose origin is not stated.

## Additional context for the board (from the public website, verbatim where quoted)

- Headline: "Coast through your content. Every client, every platform."
- Key claims: per-client brand voice profiles (tone, audience, words to use/avoid); chat-based assistant instead of templates; one workspace for all clients; save replies straight to a calendar; private data via row-level security; "human review stays in the loop".
- How it works: 1) add a client, 2) brief the assistant in chat, 3) save to the calendar and mark scheduled/published.
- What it does NOT do (per the site): no direct publishing to Instagram/TikTok/LinkedIn etc.; no image or video generation is mentioned; team seats and approval workflows are "Future" on the Agency plan only.
- Comparison table on the site claims "Others" lack per-client voices, chat assistants and calendars (no named competitors).
- Pricing: Trial €0 for 2 days (3 clients) · Starter €19/mo (5 clients) · Pro €49/mo (20 clients) · Agency €99/mo (unlimited). Monthly, cancel anytime. No annual plan shown.
- Stats on the page: "10x faster content production per client", "50+ brands managed from one workspace", "5 min to set up a new client profile". Source of these stats is not stated.
- Three named testimonials appear on the page (Laura M., Seaside Social; Tomas K., freelancer; Priya S., Brightwave Agency). Whether these are real customers is not stated.
- Unknowns: founder team, traction, AI cost per request, marketing budget, legal entity.
