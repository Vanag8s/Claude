# Brand: Coast

## 1. The name

**Recommendation: keep "Coast" as the product name** — but it needs the checks below before you spend money on it. It is short, calm, ties to the Nida/Baltic-shore story, and the verb pun ("coast through your content") is the whole brand idea in one word. Its risk: it is a common English word, so trademark and domain conflicts are likely, and it says nothing about social media.

Ten candidates considered:

| name | style | what it says | risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Coast** | evocative | calm, effortless | common word; crowded trademarks/domains |
| Coast Studio | evocative + descriptor | calm + agency-grade | longer; still "coast" conflicts |
| Kopa (LT: "dune") | place/local | Nida dunes, Baltic | means nothing outside LT; check pronunciation |
| Nida | place | the founder's place | it's a town; weak trademark |
| Voicebook | descriptive | per-client voices | generic, likely taken |
| Brandvoice | descriptive | does what it says | generic, unprotectable |
| Tidecal | invented | tide + calendar | awkward to say |
| Briefly | descriptive pun | brief → post | very likely taken |
| Kranto ("of the shore", LT) | local | shore, Lithuanian | hard for non-LT buyers |
| Approvable | descriptive | the approval loop | clunky |

**Shortlist: Coast, Coast Studio, Kopa.** Checks only you can finish (I can only run public searches; a trademark attorney confirms; nothing is protected until you register it):

- **Trademark:** EUIPO eSearch (https://euipo.europa.eu/eSearch/) and TMview (https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/) for the EU; the Lithuanian State Patent Bureau (VPB) register for LT. Classes **9** (downloadable software) and **42** (SaaS), plus **35** (advertising/marketing services) to be safe.
- **Domain:** check coast.com (almost certainly taken), getcoast.app, coast.studio, usecoast.com, coast.lt. Not checked from here.
- **Handles:** Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X for the chosen form (e.g. @coaststudio).
- **Confusion:** no competitor in `competitors.md` is named Coast. Do search "Coast" + "software" — there are likely unrelated software firms using it.

## 2. Promise, taglines, voice

**Promise:** *Every client sounds like themselves — and signs off before it goes out.*

Taglines (pick one):
1. Coast through Monday. Every client, every voice. *(recommended)*
2. On-brand for every client. Approved before it posts.
3. Your clients' voices, kept. Your Mondays, back.

**Voice: calm · specific · honest**

| adjective | do | don't |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calm | short sentences, white space, no exclamation marks | "🚀 10x your content!!!" |
| Specific | "a week of captions for 12 clients by lunch" | "supercharge your workflow" |
| Honest | say what it doesn't do ("we don't publish — we export to your scheduler") | unsourced stats, testimonials you can't back up |

Before → after (from the current site):
- Before: "Coast through your content. Every client, every platform." + "10x faster content production per client"
- After: "Coast through Monday. Write a week of on-brand posts for every client, send one approval link, export to your scheduler."

## 3. The look (brief)

The current site already has a good base (Fraunces + Inter, calm shoreline mood). Keep it; formalise it:

| colour | hex | job | text contrast |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sea ink | `#0F2A3D` | text, primary buttons | white on it ≈ 14:1 (AAA) |
| Dune sand | `#F3EDE2` | page background | sea ink on it ≈ 13:1 (AAA) |
| Baltic teal | `#1F7A7A` | accents, links | white on it ≈ 5.2:1 (AA) |

(Contrast ratios computed for these hex values; re-check if you change them.)

**Type:** Fraunces (display) + Inter (text) — both free under the SIL Open Font License on Google Fonts, which you already use.

**Logo brief:** a wordmark "coast" in Fraunces with a single low wave/horizon line; must work at 32px as an app icon (use the wave alone), on a light and a dark background, and in one colour. Avoid: palm trees, surfboards, AI sparkles, chat bubbles (every competitor uses those).

## 4. First five touchpoints (fix the trust gaps the board and panel flagged)

1. **Homepage:** own domain (not `dvai-rebranding-and-2jqh.bolt.host`), a named founder with photo and city, an "Impressum"-style company line, a data page (where data is stored, which AI provider, no training on client data — only if true). **Remove the "10x", "50+ brands" stats and the three testimonials unless they are real, sourced customers** — EU consumer-protection rules prohibit fake reviews; not legal advice.
2. **Sign-up confirmation email:** from hello@yourdomain, not Gmail; tells them how to paste 5 example posts for their first client.
3. **The client approval page** (the thing your customer's customer sees): white-label-ish, Coast logo small, agency name big.
4. **First social post:** a real before/after: one client brief → a week of captions, shown with the time it took.
5. **Reply to the first complaint:** same day, from the founder, by name.
