## The idea

- What it is: an AI content workspace for social media agencies that turns a short per-client brand brief into captions, post ideas and video scripts in each client's voice, with a per-client content calendar.
- Who it is for: small social media agencies and freelance social media managers who run content for several client brands at once (roughly 3 to 50 brands), mainly in Europe (euro pricing).
- What it sells, at what price: a monthly SaaS subscription. Free trial 2 days, no card, up to 3 clients. Starter €19/month (5 clients), Pro €49/month (20 clients, "most popular"), Agency €99/month (unlimited clients; team seats and approvals listed as "future"). Unlimited AI requests on every paid plan.
- Where and how: online, self-serve web app (built on Bolt, currently at dvai-rebranding-and-2jqh.bolt.host; contact coastagencyai@gmail.com). It does not post to social networks itself: users copy the text into their own scheduler or save it to Coast's calendar. Brand story references Nida, Lithuania.
- Budget and constraints: not stated by the founder. Not yet known: marketing budget, the AI model and its per-request cost, current users or revenue, team size. The site shows three testimonials and stats ("10x faster", "50+ brands") whose origin is not stated.

### 5. The gap

There is **no gap in "AI captions per client in English"** — it is crowded and the $20 general assistant covers most of it. Two gaps the evidence supports:

1. **Truly unlimited, predictable AI for many brands at a flat price.** Competitors meter credits and that is the most repeated complaint. Coast already promises unlimited — this is a real angle *if the CFO says the cost per request allows it*.
2. **Voice fidelity + approvals for small, non-English (e.g. Lithuanian / Baltic) agencies.** None of the listed tools positions on local-language voice quality; all are English-first US/UK products. This is a hypothesis, not a finding — no source was found either way. It is the board's "small market to own first".

### 6. The threat

Fastest to copy: **any scheduler adding "brand voice per workspace"** (Ocoya, FeedHive, SocialBee already have AI + workspaces), and **ChatGPT/Claude themselves** as project features improve. A Bolt-built app has no technical moat; the moat has to be the niche, the voice data (example posts per client) and the approval workflow.

- **AI cost:** if real AI cost is 3× the estimate (€12 instead of €4 per subscriber — plausible for heavy Agency users), contribution falls to €14.24 and break-even rises to 27. Measure it in week 1. A fair-use cap (~1,500 requests) protects this.
- **Three ways to improve it (each run through the tool):**
  1. **Cut paid marketing to €0 until a pilot proves demand** → break-even **4 subscribers**, year 1 **€4,022**. Founder-led outreach costs time, not cash.
  2. **Raise the blended price to €35** (e.g. more agencies on Studio/Agency) → break-even **13**, year 1 **€2,169**.

- **Board's money conditions:** AI cost per request — still an estimate, **not met**. Fair-use cap — added to the offer, **met on paper**. Positive margin per tier — met, *if* AI cost is near the estimate.
- **Founder pay is €0 in this model.** Any salary pushes break-even up by (salary ÷ €22.24) subscribers — e.g. €1,500/month needs ~67 more subscribers.
