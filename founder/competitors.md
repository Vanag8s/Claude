## 2. Price range for "AI content for ~5 to 20 client brands"

- Lowest: **$20/mo** (general AI assistant, substitute) · €16–29/mo (Metricool, AI metered).
- Median of the direct/indirect tools: **~$40–60/mo** for 5–20 brands (Ocoya Silver $39, SocialBee $49, Cloud Campaign $49, Planable $59 per workspace).
- Highest: $249/mo (Predis unlimited) to $499/mo (Sendible bundles).

Coast at €19 / €49 / €99 sits **at the low-to-middle** of the market — while doing less (no publishing, no visuals, no approvals).

## 4. What their customers complain about (ranked; all themes are **thin** — under 3 independent reviews each in what search returned)

1. **AI credits run out / unpredictable usage** (Predis, Ocoya lower tiers). "credits are spent even when the AI suggests something off-topic" — Predis reviewer, [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/231932/Predisai/reviews/). Thin.
2. **Output still sounds generic; needs heavy editing.** AI content "still requires human review and editing to ensure brand accuracy and avoid generic-sounding copy" — [aitoolsatlas](https://aitoolsatlas.ai/tools/predis-ai/pros-cons); Ocoya reviewers "would like to see AI outputs improve" — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/232714/Ocoya/reviews/?page=7). Thin.
3. **Per-workspace pricing adds up for agencies** (Planable: 15 workspaces ≈ $585/mo before add-ons) — [cloudcampaign vs planable](https://www.cloudcampaign.com/lp/vs-planable) (a competitor's framing). Thin.
4. **Approvals gated behind higher tiers** (Planable Basic: optional approvals only) — same source. Thin.

Practitioner guides agree on one thing: **real past posts as examples beat tone adjectives** for getting a client's voice ([velocity](https://www.velocity.li/blog/why-ai-captions-sound-robotic), [cloudcampaign answers](https://www.cloudcampaign.com/answers/ai-captions-in-brand-voice)). Coast's profile today stores tone/audience/words, not example posts — per the site.

## 5. The gap

There is **no gap in "AI captions per client in English"** — it is crowded and the $20 general assistant covers most of it. Two gaps the evidence supports:

1. **Truly unlimited, predictable AI for many brands at a flat price.** Competitors meter credits and that is the most repeated complaint. Coast already promises unlimited — this is a real angle *if the CFO says the cost per request allows it*.
2. **Voice fidelity + approvals for small, non-English (e.g. Lithuanian / Baltic) agencies.** None of the listed tools positions on local-language voice quality; all are English-first US/UK products. This is a hypothesis, not a finding — no source was found either way. It is the board's "small market to own first".

## 6. The threat

Fastest to copy: **any scheduler adding "brand voice per workspace"** (Ocoya, FeedHive, SocialBee already have AI + workspaces), and **ChatGPT/Claude themselves** as project features improve. A Bolt-built app has no technical moat; the moat has to be the niche, the voice data (example posts per client) and the approval workflow.
