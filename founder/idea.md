# Coast

- What it is: an AI content SaaS that turns a short brand brief into captions, post ideas and video scripts in each client's own voice, with a per-client content calendar to review, approve and schedule.
- Who it is for: social media agencies and freelance social media managers who run content for several client brands at once (from solo freelancers to ~20-person teams).
- What it sells, at what price: monthly subscription. Free trial 2 days, no card, up to 3 clients. Starter €19/month (5 clients), Pro €49/month (20 clients, priority speed), Agency €99/month (unlimited clients; team seats and approvals listed as "future").
- Where and how: online, priced in euros (Europe-facing; brand imagery references Nida, Lithuania). Delivered as a web app built on Bolt with sign-in and row-level security. Acquisition channel not yet stated.
- Budget and constraints: not stated. Stage not stated (no evidence given of paying customers).
