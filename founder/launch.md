# Launch: Coast

## 1. Test before you spend: a paid concierge pilot

Business type: service/SaaS → **paid pilot clients at a real price.** Both panels said the same thing: *"show me it working on my own clients."* So do exactly that, by hand if needed.

- **Who:** 5 Lithuanian/Baltic freelancers or small agencies (from direct outreach; see `marketing.md`).
- **What:** for 4 weeks, each sends you 2–3 real clients' past posts and briefs; Coast (plus you, by hand where features aren't built) delivers their Monday batch and a client approval link.
- **Price:** **€19/month, paid up front** for the pilot month (the founding price). Free pilots prove nothing.
- **Measure:** edit rate per post, client approval rate, minutes spent per client-week vs. their current way, whether they pay for month 2.
- **Success line (write it down now):** **≥ 3 of 5 pay for month 2, and ≥ 60% of posts approved with no or light edits.**
- **Compare with the panel:** the panel predicted 0%. If real buyers beat it, trust the real buyers. If they don't, stop building features and go back to `/founder-offer` or rethink the market (e.g. sell approvals to agencies, or sell done-for-you Lithuanian content as a service).

## 4. First 30 days

Weekly: trials started, trial→paid (alarm < 10%), paying subscribers vs. **break-even of 18** (4 without paid marketing), edit rate (alarm < 50%), AI cost per subscriber (alarm > €8), cancel reasons.

- **Day 7:** Which channel brought paying users? Where do trials drop off?
- **Day 14:** Is the edit rate holding beyond the first client? Any account above fair use?
- **Day 30:** Paying count vs. 18. Month-2 retention of pilot users. Decide: keep the niche, widen it, or change the offer. Then re-run `/founder-cfo` with real numbers.
