# Marketing: Coast

**Evidence it's built on:** two simulated panels (0/20 buy each — no segment bought, so there is *no proven first audience yet*), the competitor map, the offer. So this plan is built to **find** the first audience cheaply, not to scale one. Panel quotes are research only — never use them as customer quotes in ads.

## 1. Positioning

1. **(Recommended)** For **Lithuanian and Baltic social media freelancers and small agencies**, who *"lose most of Sunday evening writing captions"* and chase clients on WhatsApp for approval, **Coast** is the content workspace that **writes each client's week in their own voice — in Lithuanian or English — and gets it approved with one link**, unlike ChatGPT, which forgets the client and leaves approvals in your inbox.
   *Evidence:* Lithuanian quality and approvals were the most specific flip conditions in both panels (P009, P011, P019); no competitor in `competitors.md` positions on local language.
2. For freelancers with 5–15 clients, Coast is the **approval loop** for social posts — draft, client approves on their phone, export to your scheduler. *Evidence:* the approval link is the one feature buyers called valuable on its own (v2 P018, P013).
3. For small agencies, Coast is **flat-price unlimited AI** across clients, unlike credit-metered tools. *Evidence:* credit limits are the top competitor complaint (thin). Weak against the €20 general assistant.

## 4. Ten hooks (each answers one objection)

1. "Your ChatGPT forgets your client every Monday. Coast doesn't." *(habit)*
2. "Lithuanian captions that don't sound translated." *(language)*
3. "Send one link. Your client approves 7 posts from WhatsApp." *(approvals)*
4. "Not one more tab: export the approved week straight to your scheduler." *(one more tab)*
5. "Teach it a voice with 5 real posts, not 5 adjectives." *(generic output)*
6. "14 days on your real clients. No card." *(trial too short)*
7. "If your client rejects the voice, your first month is free." *(risk)*
8. "Built by one person in Nida. Here's my face, my phone, and where your data lives." *(trust)*
9. "From 4 hours on Sunday to 40 minutes on Monday." *(only once a pilot user actually measured it)*
10. "€19 a month for 15 clients — for the first 50 only." *(price)*

## 5. Budget and numbers

- **Budget: €0 paid until 20 paying subscribers.** The CFO shows the €300/month marketing line moves break-even from 4 to 18 subscribers. Spend on a meetup sponsorship (~€100) only if a group offers one.
- **Most you can pay to win a customer:** contribution €22.24/month × assumed average life of 10 months (10% monthly churn — an assumption, measure it) ≈ **€222 lifetime contribution**; keep CAC under a third → **≤ €74 per paying customer**.
- **Watch weekly:** (1) trial → paid conversion (alarm < 10%), (2) **edit rate** — share of drafts approved with no or light edits (alarm < 50%), (3) month-2 retention (alarm < 70%).
