habit 11 · trust 6 · need 2 · quality 1. In plain words: *"I already have ChatGPT/Claude with a project per client"*, *"it doesn't publish or do approvals, so it's one more tab"*, *"who is behind this?"*, *"2 days isn't enough"*, *"will it write Lithuanian?"*.

## 2. Problem list (buyer's words, from the panel + competitor complaints)

Before buying: 1) I already pay for ChatGPT. 2) Another subscription. 3) Looks like a side project. 4) Where is my clients' data? 5) 2 days is too short. 6) €49 for 3–8 clients is too much. 7) Every tool says "brand voice" and it's still generic. 8) Will it do Lithuanian?
During: 9) Re-entering 12 client voices is work. 10) Copy-pasting into my scheduler. 11) Chasing clients for approval on WhatsApp. 12) Screenshots of posts for approval. 13) Messy briefs arrive as Google Docs. 14) Sunday nights lost to captions. 15) Credits running out (competitors).
After: 16) Calendar goes stale if I tick "published" by hand. 17) What if a client hates the voice? 18) What if the tool disappears? 19) Team members can't log in. 20) Can I take my content with me?

## 4. The stack — "The Monday Batch"

- **Core:** a week of on-brand posts for every client, approved by the client, ready for your scheduler — before lunch on Monday.
- **Bonus 1:** client approval link (kills "chasing on WhatsApp").
- **Bonus 2:** one-click CSV export for your scheduler (kills "one more tab").
- **Bonus 3:** founder-led voice setup for your first 3 clients (founding members only).
- **Guarantee:** if a client rejects the voice in your first paid month, that month is refunded.
- **Urgency (true):** founding-member price for the first 50 accounts only.
- **Name:** *The Monday Batch* (the offer), sold by Coast (the company).

**Must be built before it is sold:** approval link, CSV export, example-post voice learning. None of these exist today per the website. Do not advertise them until they work.

## 6. Re-test (same 20 buyers, seed 42)

| | v1 pitch | v2 pitch (this offer) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Buy | **0 / 20** | **0 / 20** |
| habit ("I already have ChatGPT") | 11 | 10 |
| trust | 6 | 4 |
| need ("one more tab") | 2 | 1 |
| quality ("prove the voice") | 1 | 5 |
| Acceptable price range | €9–49 | €9–40 |

**Nothing moved the buy rate.** The objections shifted: fewer "one more tab" and "who is this" answers, more "prove it on my clients". The approval link came up several times as the one feature people liked ("He would pay for the approval link on its own" — P018 v2). Per the method, when a better offer moves nothing, **the problem is the market position, not the offer**: buyers already solve this with a €20 general assistant. The only flip condition almost all 20 named is *seeing it work on their own real clients*. That points to a hands-on pilot, not more features (see `launch.md`).
