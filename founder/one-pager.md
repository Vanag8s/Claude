# Coast: one page

**The problem.** Freelancers and small agencies who run social media for 5 to 20 brands spend hours each week writing captions in each client's voice, then chase clients over WhatsApp to approve them.

**The customer.** Lithuanian and Baltic social media freelancers and agencies of 1 to 10 people, each with 3 to 30 client brands. They write in Lithuanian and English.

**The offer and price: "The Monday Batch."** Coast learns each client's voice from 5 to 10 of their real posts. It writes the week's posts, the client approves them with one link, and the approved posts export to the agency's scheduler. Plans are Solo €15, **Studio €29**, and Agency €59 a month. The first 50 founding members get Studio at €19, locked for 12 months. Trial is 14 days with no card. If a client rejects the voice, the first paid month is refunded. *The approval link, the CSV export and learning from example posts must be built first.*

**The numbers** (euros, projections from estimates):

**The evidence, plainly.**
- **Panel:** 0 of 20 simulated buyers bought, both on the current offer and on the rebuilt one. The main reason is that they already use ChatGPT or Claude with a project per client. Nearly all of them said what would change their mind: seeing it work on their own clients.
- **Competitor gap:** none of the tools reviewed positions on local-language voice quality, and approvals cost extra in most of them ($59 per workspace on Planable). This is a hypothesis.
- **Real test:** none yet.

**What the money is for.** About €3,700 covers company and trademark setup, legal templates (privacy policy and DPA), launch costs and early losses. That money buys a 4-week paid pilot with 5 agencies.

**The ask.** No investment is being sought yet. The ask is for **5 agencies to join a €19 paid pilot** between 30 Nov and 27 Dec 2026. Go or no-go: at least 3 of 5 pay for month 2, and at least 60% of posts are approved with no or light edits.

*The panel is simulated buyers and the numbers are projections. Not financial, legal or tax advice.*
