## What would flip a no

- Team seats and client approvals actually shipped, plus a trial with 20 clients where I could see the captions needing almost no rewriting on my hardest client.
- A direct integration that pushes saved captions into my scheduler (Metricool or Buffer) as drafts, so I don't copy and paste.
- Client approvals and publishing straight to my scheduler, so it replaces a step in my workflow instead of duplicating ChatGPT. Also a trial that lets me run a real Sunday batch for all my clients.
- If during the 2-day trial it gave me captions in a client's voice that I could post with almost no edits, and the plan covering my clients cost around 20 a month.
- If it pushed captions straight into my scheduler, or replaced the scheduler, and the free trial lasted long enough to run a full week of client captions.
- A two-day trial isn't enough. I'd need to run a real Monday of client posts through it and have them approved without edits, plus a clear statement of who the company is and what happens to my client data.
- Publishing or scheduling straight to the networks plus client approvals working today (not 'coming later'), so it replaces my scheduler and not just my AI chat.
- Direct publishing to my scheduler and client approvals built in, so it replaces a tool I pay for instead of adding one.
- Proof in the 2-day trial that it writes clearly better Lithuanian than my current setup, plus client approvals that work over WhatsApp or email.
- Seeing real output in a client's voice that I couldn't tell from my own writing, plus approvals and publishing built in so it replaces something I already pay for.
- Proof that it writes good Lithuanian, a real company behind it with a track record, and team seats plus client approvals actually shipped. A longer trial on my own clients would also help.
- Client approvals that work over WhatsApp or email screenshots, or publishing straight to my scheduler, so it replaces a tool I pay for instead of adding one.
