## What would flip a no

- Seeing it produce a week of captions for two of my real clients during the trial that I'd send with under a few minutes of edits, plus a founding price that covers 25+ clients.
- A free trial where I run two real clients and the clients approve most posts untouched, plus a CSV that imports into my scheduler without me fixing columns.
- If the approval link plus the CSV export visibly cut my Sunday evening from hours to under an hour on my real clients during the free trial, and the voice matched without me tweaking every post.
- If in the trial a client approved a full week of captions with almost no edits from me, and I could see it was clearly better than what I get from the free AI tools.
- If the free trial showed me a full week of captions for 3 of my real clients that needed almost no editing and the client approved them from WhatsApp, and the plan replaced a tool I already pay for instead of adding one.
- Seeing a sample week for one of my real clients where the client approved nearly every post with no edits, or a trusted agency owner I know vouching for it.
- Seeing it match a client's voice clearly better than my Claude project on two of my real clients in the trial, and the approval link plus CSV import into my scheduler working with no manual fixing.
- Proof that it replaces something I already pay for (e.g. my scheduler's approval step or a writing tool) and cuts my Monday batch time by hours across 20+ clients, shown on my own clients during the free trial.
- Seeing real Lithuanian captions from the trial that clearly beat what I get from Claude today, plus a client actually approving through the link without me explaining anything.
- Seeing it nail the voice of two of my own clients in the free trial, with me only editing lightly, plus an approval flow that replaces my chasing clients and doesn't add a second tool.
- A real Lithuanian-language sample made from one of my clients' own posts that is clearly better than my Claude project, plus a few named agencies using it.
- A trial on two of my real clients where the drafts clearly sound more like them than my current Claude prompts, and the clients actually use the approval link without me chasing them. Then 19 a month would be an easy yes.
