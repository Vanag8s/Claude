Coast: an AI workspace for people who run social media for several client brands.
Save each client's tone, audience and words to use or avoid once; then chat to get captions, post ideas and Reels/TikTok scripts in that client's voice, and save them to a per-client content calendar.
It does not publish to social networks: you copy the text into your scheduler. Client approvals and team seats are "coming later".
Prices (euros per month, cancel anytime): Starter 19 (5 clients), Pro 49 (20 clients, most popular), Agency 99 (unlimited). Unlimited AI requests on all plans.
Free trial: 2 days, no card, up to 3 clients.
