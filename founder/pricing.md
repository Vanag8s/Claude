## 2. What competitors charge (`competitors.md`, third-party figures — verify)

The real anchor is **€20/month for ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro** that most buyers already pay. Schedulers with AI: Metricool €16–29, Ocoya $39 (5 workspaces), SocialBee $29–99, FeedHive ~$29. A tool that *adds* to the €20 they already pay is compared to €0; a tool that *replaces* a step they pay for can charge €15–35.

## 3. What the business needs (`cfo.md`)

Contribution ~€22 per subscriber at €27 blended; break-even 18 subscribers with €300/mo marketing, 4 without.

## 4. Decision

**The price:** a three-rung ladder, all inside the acceptable range, with the "most popular" rung near the IPP:

| plan | price/mo | clients | why step up |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Solo | €15 | 5 | freelancers starting out |
| **Studio** | **€29** | 15 | most freelancers/small agencies hit 6–15 clients |
| Agency | €59 | unlimited + 3 seats | team seats + approvals for every client |

Annual billing: 2 months free (cash up front, lower churn).

**Opening offer:** *Founding member* — first 50 accounts get Studio at **€19/month locked for 12 months**. Real scarcity (a counted list), ends when 50 are taken or on a fixed date, whichever first. No "was €X" anchors.

**What to test with real buyers:** Studio at **€19 vs €29** — two versions of the pilot invite (see `launch.md`), measure paid conversions after the trial, not sign-ups.
