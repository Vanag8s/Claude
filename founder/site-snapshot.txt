FEATURES

Built for the way agencies actually work.

Most AI tools serve one brand. Coast is designed for teams juggling many — with per-client voice, calendars and a chat assistant that remembers who it's writing for.

AI assistant that writes like your brand

Chat naturally to get captions, post ideas and video scripts. The assistant reads each client’s saved voice and audience so every reply sounds right — not generic.

Every client in one workspace

Switch between brands in a click. Each one keeps its own voice profile, content calendar and history, so nothing bleeds between accounts.

Brand voice that actually sticks

Save tone, audience, words to use and avoid. The assistant applies it to every request, so a dental clinic doesn’t sound like a streetwear label.

Content calendar at a glance

Every piece lives on a per-client calendar. Set dates, mark drafts as scheduled or published, and see the whole month at a glance.

One-click copy and save

Copy any reply to paste into your scheduler, or save it straight to the calendar with a title and date in one click.

Private to your account

Your clients and content are locked behind your sign-in. Row-level security means no one else can reach your data — not even another Coast user.

HOW IT WORKS

From brief to scheduled content in three steps.

No prompt engineering, no copy-paste between tools. Coast sits between your brief and your scheduler.

1
Add a client

Save each brand name, voice and audience once. Coast remembers it for every future request.

2
Brief the assistant

Chat naturally — ask for a week of captions, ten post ideas or a Reels script. Replies match the brand voice.

3
Save and schedule

Send any reply straight to the content calendar with one click. Set a date, mark it scheduled, done.
