# Summary

**Verdict: NOT YET.** What has to change first: **demand.** 0 of 20 simulated buyers bought — on today's offer *and* on a rebuilt one. Before building more, run a paid 5-agency pilot (`launch.md`); if it misses its success line, rethink the market with `/founder-offer`.

**What it is.** Coast is an AI workspace that writes captions, post ideas and video scripts in each client's saved voice, for freelancers and small agencies who run social media for several brands. Today it sells at €19 / €49 / €99 a month from a Bolt-hosted site.

**The three numbers** (euros; every input is an estimate, see `cfo-sources.md`):
- **Margin:** €22.24 of each €27.20 subscriber-month is left after AI and fees (82%). Software margins are not the problem.
- **Break-even:** 18 paying subscribers with €300/month marketing; **4** with none.
- **Year 1:** €422 operating profit on an *assumed* ramp of 2 → 40 subscribers, −€2,028 after startup costs. The panel gives that ramp no support.

**Where the board and the panel agree:** buyers already get "AI captions per client" from a €20 ChatGPT/Claude project, so Coast is "one more tab". The site's unsourced stats and testimonials hurt trust. Approvals and a scheduler hand-off are missing.
**Where they disagree:** two board lenses see an offer that can be fixed (FUND IF). The Monopoly lens passes because there is no secret. The panel sides with the Monopoly lens so far: a better offer moved the reasons for passing but not the buy rate.

**Biggest risk:** no one switches from the general AI assistant they already pay for. **What to do about it:** compete where that assistant is weak. That means a client approval link, Lithuanian-language voice quality, and proof on real clients. Prove it with 5 paying pilots before spending on ads.

**What the founder needs to start:** about **€3,700** in cash (CFO) and 4 weeks of hands-on pilot work. Not financial advice.

**This week:** take the unsourced "10x / 50+ brands" stats and the three testimonials off the site, then send 25 personal messages to Lithuanian freelancers and agencies offering the €19 paid pilot.

*The panel is simulated buyers and the numbers are projections. Real customers and real quotes confirm them. Not financial, legal or tax advice.*
