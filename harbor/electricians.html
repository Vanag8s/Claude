Questions from electrical owners

What about dangerous situations?

For burning smells, sparks or shocks, Harbor tells the caller to cut power at the panel if it's safe and call 911 if there's fire, then alerts you.

Can it book EV charger installs?

Yes. It asks for the car, the panel size and where the charger goes, and books the estimate.

Can it screen tiny jobs?

You decide the minimum job size or trip fee and Harbor explains it upfront.
