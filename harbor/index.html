        FAQ

        Questions people
        actually ask.

        Does it really sound like a person?

        It sounds warm and natural, replies almost instantly, and stops talking the moment the caller does, just like a person would. It always says it's a virtual assistant. Try it above: talk to it, or have it call you.

        Can it really tell my mood?

        On calls, Harbor picks up on how people sound from what they say and how they say it: rushed, worried, annoyed or chatty. It adapts, keeping things short, slowing down, or putting them through to you. In the website demo you can switch on "Show what Harbor senses" and watch it work.

        Who is Harbor for?

        Any business that misses calls: solo trades and salons, clinics and offices, and multi-location teams. Shore suits small businesses, Tide growing ones, and Ocean big teams.

        How long does setup take?

        Usually under 48 hours. We build your Harbor from your website and a short call, test it with you, and then you forward your calls.

        Do I need to change my phone number?

        No. Keep your number and forward calls to Harbor: all of them, only when you're busy, or only after hours.

        What if it doesn't work for us?

        If Harbor doesn't catch at least 30 calls you would have missed in your first 30 days, the first month is free. There's no contract, so cancel any time.

        Can it speak other languages?

        Yes. Harbor can detect the caller's language and answer in it. Tell us which languages your customers speak.

        What happens to my customers' data?

        Encrypted in transit and at rest, never sold, and never used to train public AI models. See our Privacy Policy.
