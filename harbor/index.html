FAQ

Questions owners
actually ask.

Will customers know they're talking to AI?

Harbor introduces itself as your virtual assistant. It sounds natural and friendly, but we never pretend it's a human — honesty builds trust and keeps you compliant with disclosure laws.

How long does setup take, and what do I have to do?

7 days. You do one 45-minute kickoff call. We build your knowledge base, pricing rules, voice, and calendar sync, then run test calls with you before going live.

Will it quote wrong prices or promise things I can't do?

No. Harbor only quotes from the price ranges you approve and says "a technician will confirm on site" for anything else. Every conversation is logged so you can review it.

Do I need to change my phone number?

No. Keep your number and forward overflow or after-hours calls to Harbor. Takes two minutes with any carrier.

What software does it work with?

Google Calendar, Outlook, Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, and most CRMs via Zapier. If you use pen and paper, Harbor texts you every booking.

What if it doesn't work for my business?

The 10 Jobs Guarantee: if Harbor doesn't book at least 10 extra jobs in 60 days, you get a full refund and we keep working free until it does. No contracts, cancel any time.

What happens to my customers' data?

Encrypted in transit and at rest, never sold, never used to train public AI models. See our Privacy Policy.
