Our goal

We want everyone to be able to use the Harbor website, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader, zoom or voice control. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 level AA.

What we've done

  • Text and buttons meet AA colour contrast (at least 4.5:1).
  • Everything works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to content" link.
  • All form fields have labels, error messages are announced, and buttons say what they do.
  • Images have text alternatives; decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Animations stop if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • The chat can be used by typing; the microphone and voice are optional.

Known limitations

  • The sample call recordings don't have a written transcript on the page yet. A transcript is available on request.
  • Voice features depend on your browser; typing always works.

Feedback

If something doesn't work for you, email [accessibility email]. We'll reply within 5 working days.
