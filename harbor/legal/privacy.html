3. AI and automated replies

Harbor is an AI system. It tells callers and visitors that it's a virtual assistant. Replies are generated automatically and can occasionally be wrong; members approve what Harbor may say, and every call has a transcript that can be checked. Harbor doesn't make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Who processes data for us

We use these service providers (processors), each under a data processing agreement:

  • AI: Anthropic (Claude), or Google (Gemini) if configured. We use their paid business services, which don't use your data to train their models.
  • Telephony and text messages: Twilio.
  • Website hosting: Vercel. Voice server hosting: Fly.io. Database: Upstash.
  • Payments: Stripe. Analytics (with consent only): Google Analytics.

Some providers are based outside the EU/EEA. Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. How long we keep data

  • Sign-up and lead information: up to 24 months after our last contact.
  • Call transcripts and summaries for members: for as long as the member's account is active plus 90 days, unless the member sets a shorter period.
  • Demo call numbers: up to 30 days, to prevent abuse.
  • Invoices: as long as accounting law requires.

6. Call recording notice

Harbor tells callers at the start of a call that it's a virtual assistant and that the call may be recorded or transcribed. Members must make sure this notice meets the rules in their country.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email [privacy email]. We reply within one month.

You can also complain to your data protection authority. In Lithuania that's the State Data Protection Inspectorate (Valstybinė duomenų apsaugos inspekcija, vdai.lrv.lt).

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access is limited to people who need it, and each member's settings are protected by a private admin key.

9. Changes

We'll post any changes here and update the date above. For important changes we'll tell members by email.
