1. Who we are

Harbor ("we", "us") provides AI front-desk software for home-service businesses. Contact: privacy@harbor-ai.example.

2. What we collect

  • Website visitors: information you submit in forms (name, business, email, phone, trade) and, only with your consent, analytics data about pages visited.
  • Demo chat: messages you type into the demo assistant, processed to generate replies. Do not enter sensitive personal data.
  • Clients and their customers: call recordings, transcripts, messages and booking details processed on our clients' behalf.

3. How we use it

To deliver the audit you request, operate and improve the service, provide support, send service messages, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use client or customer data to train public AI models.

4. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Consent (analytics cookies, marketing), contract (providing the service), and legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, product improvement).

5. Processors

We use vetted sub-processors for hosting, AI model inference, telephony, email and analytics, bound by data-processing agreements. A current list is available on request.

6. Retention

Lead information: up to 24 months after last contact. Client call data: for the life of the contract plus 90 days, unless the client sets a shorter period.

7. Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, portability, or object to processing by emailing us. California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA; we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

8. Security

Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, role-based access, and audit logging.

9. Changes

We will post updates here and change the "last updated" date.
