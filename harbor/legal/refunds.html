Cancel any time

Harbor is a monthly or yearly membership with no long contract. You can cancel any time from the billing link in your welcome email or by emailing [support email]. Your membership stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.

The "pays off or it's free" promise (first 30 days)

If Harbor doesn't answer at least 30 calls that you would otherwise have missed during your first 30 days of live service, we refund your first month's membership fee.

  • "Calls you would have missed" means calls Harbor answered because they were forwarded to it (overflow, after-hours, or all calls), as shown in your call log.
  • Call forwarding must stay switched on for the whole 30 days.
  • Ask for the refund by email within 14 days after the 30 days end. We refund to the original payment method within 10 working days.
  • The promise applies once per business.

Other refunds

  • We don't refund partly used months or years, except under the promise above or where the law requires it.
  • If we charged you by mistake or Harbor was unavailable for a long time because of us, contact us and we'll make it right.
  • Yearly memberships cancelled within the first 30 days are refunded in full, minus any month already refunded under the promise.

Consumers

Harbor is meant for businesses. If you are a consumer in the EU, you have a 14-day right to withdraw from the contract. If you asked us to start the service within those 14 days, we may charge for the part already provided.
