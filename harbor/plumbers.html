Questions from plumbing owners

Will it give safety advice?

Yes. For leaks and floods Harbor tells callers how to shut off the water before anything else, and it flags the call as urgent.

Can it quote my prices?

It quotes only the price ranges you approve, e.g. drain cleaning $149–$249, and says the tech confirms on site.

What about gas smells?

Harbor tells the caller to leave the building and call the gas company or 911 from outside, then alerts you.
