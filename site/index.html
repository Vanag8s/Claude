FAQ

Questions agencies ask first.

I already use ChatGPT or Claude. Why Coast?

Keep them for brainstorming. Coast is the workflow around the writing: a saved voice and calendar per client, a week per brief, an approval link for the client and an export for your scheduler. It's the part a chat window leaves to you.

Does Coast publish to Instagram or TikTok?

No — on purpose. You keep the scheduler you already trust. Coast fills it with approved posts via CSV export.

How good is it in Lithuanian?

Coast writes natively in Lithuanian and English. Judge it on your own clients: the 14-day trial is long enough to run two real Monday batches.

Do my clients need an account to approve?

No. They open the link on their phone, tap approve or leave a comment on each post.

What happens to my content if I leave?

Export everything as CSV before you go. After you close your account, your data is deleted.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans stop at the end of the period; there's nothing to negotiate.
